AI, GDPR and information security as one package

AI systems process personal data and rely on secure infrastructure. Regulation, privacy and security therefore have to be assessed together, not in separate silos. I cover the whole chain, which avoids duplicate work and closes the gaps between teams.

AI governance

EU AI Act, ISO 42001, FRIA and risk-based assessment, with clear guardrails that enable AI adoption instead of slowing it down.

GDPR

Data protection requirements for AI use cases: lawful basis, transparency, data flows, records of processing activities (RoPA / VVT) and data protection impact assessments (DPIA / DSFA).

Information security

Technical and organizational measures (TOMs), vendor risk and controls under NIS2 and DORA that protect AI systems and the data behind them.



What I deliver

  • Compliance audits End-to-end review along the AI, GDPR and security chain, with findings and a clear action plan.
  • AI register (KI-Register) Inventory of AI systems with purpose, risk class and responsible owners, as the basis for AI Act compliance.
  • Legal reviews Contracts, vendors and AI use cases assessed for legal, privacy and security risk.
  • Legal tech automation Python-based workflows that turn these checks into repeatable, documented processes.
